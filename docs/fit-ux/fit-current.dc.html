Summary

This announcement invites applications to study the mechanisms by which immune signalling in peripheral and central nervous tissue initiates, amplifies or resolves chronic pain in human participants. Studies must include human subjects; mechanistic readouts in tissue, cells or imaging derived from those participants are responsive. Clinical trials, epidemiologic surveys and implementation studies are not responsive under this announcement.
